Space Ranker Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy covers the Android game Space Ranker and its internal test features. You can play the core game offline. Game saves are stored on your device and, if you connect Google Play Games, in your Google cloud save. Google Play Games provides the optional online profile.

Data on your device

The game uses Android app storage for your progress, ships, upgrades, settings, and unfinished expeditions. It checks the integrity of local saves. The game also stores your service choices, records needed to verify rewards or purchases, and the age group you select, such as 16–17.

Google Play Games and purchases

If you connect Google Play Games, the game uses your Play Games player ID and profile information to identify your profile. Google can store your cloud save and process your scores and achievements. Your Play Games profile name and submitted scores may appear on leaderboards according to your Play Games settings.

Google Play processes the one-time purchases available for internal testing. The game receives purchase details such as product identifiers, purchase status, and tokens to grant or restore items. Payment details are handled by Google Play.

Test ads and optional analytics

The internal test uses Google AdMob demo ads and requests non-personalized ads. Ad requests require an age group of 16 or older. Google’s User Messaging Platform checks which ad privacy options apply. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address, app and ad interactions, device or app identifiers, and technical diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Usage analytics is off by default. If you turn on Allow optional usage analytics in Privacy, and your age group and applicable Google consent settings allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase can process app opens, sessions, app and device details, an app-instance identifier, and supported purchase events such as a product and price. Shadedkin Studios uses these reports to understand how the game is used. You can turn the switch off at any time to stop future usage analytics collection.

Read the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data in its services.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Use Account → Start over to begin a new local adventure. Recovery copies may remain on the device. If a Google cloud adventure is linked, you can also select Also delete my Google cloud adventure. You can clear Space Ranker’s storage in Android settings to remove the app’s local data, including recovery copies and settings.
  • Use the game’s account settings to manage your Google Play Games connection. Use Google’s account settings to manage Play Games activity and data Google retains. Google Play retains purchase history separately from your game save.
  • Use Privacy → Advertising privacy choices when Google makes that option available. The analytics switch controls future collection. Analytics reports already processed and other Google service data are subject to Google’s retention controls.

If you contact the studio, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond and resolve your request. We retain those messages as needed for support. For privacy questions or a request to access or delete information held by the studio, email shadedkinstudios@gmail.com.
