Dungeon Ranker pre-registration reward terms

Shadedkin Studios offers 1,000 bonus Sigils to players who pre-register for Dungeon Ranker on Google Play while the offer is available in their Play country or region. The offer is limited to one reward per eligible Google Play account. No purchase is required.

To receive the reward, install Dungeon Ranker from Google Play after release using the same Google Play account you used to pre-register, then open the game. When the game receives the eligible reward from Google Play, it adds 1,000 Sigils to your Dungeon Ranker balance and shows a confirmation. Use the Dungeon Ranker account on which you want to receive the reward.

Bonus Sigils can be used in Dungeon Ranker. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. If the reward does not appear, contact Shadedkin Studios at shadedkinstudios@gmail.com.
