Dungeon Ranker Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Shadedkin Studios operates Dungeon Ranker and is responsible for the personal information it processes as described here. For privacy questions, email shadedkinstudios@gmail.com.

If you choose to pre-register, Google Play records your interest in the game. Your Studio-hosted game account is created through Play Games sign-in inside Dungeon Ranker. Pre-registration on the store listing alone does not create that account. Dungeon Ranker offers no public pre-registration bonus.

Dungeon Ranker stores your adventure on your device and connects to Google Play Games for cloud saves, rankings and achievements. The Android version requires an internet connection and Google Play Games sign-in during first setup. Ordinary gameplay also requires internet after setup. Permanent Patron's Crest owners can play offline. An active Ranker's Pass includes offline play for up to 72 hours after Google Play verification. Internet is needed to purchase or restore these benefits on a device. The game reads Android's connection status to apply this rule; it does not send that status to a Shadedkin Studios server. Google Play purchases are optional, and advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads when available. Builds configured for ad testing use test advertisements; other builds may request live advertisements when Google makes them available. Releases that include Google Analytics for Firebase use it for app-usage and advertising measurement as described below. Signing in with Google Play Games connects you to a Studio-hosted Dungeon Ranker account used for purchase verification and refunds. We do not operate a custom analytics backend or use Firebase Authentication or Firebase Crashlytics.

Information and purposes

Your local save includes heroes, equipment, currencies, gameplay progress, settings and records needed to avoid granting a purchase or reward twice. Device-local records also limit advertisement frequency and record pending ad rewards. These records are used to operate the game and recover interrupted actions. An advertising age group is stored only on this device; we do not ask for your birthday. The advertising SDK receives the applicable teen/adult treatment when ads are permitted, rather than an exact age or birth date.

When Google Play Games is used, Google may provide a player identifier and display name. Cloud backup sends a game-progress snapshot to Google Play Games. The game uses this identity to keep cloud choices associated with the correct player. When connected, earned leaderboard scores and achievement progress may also be sent to Google Play Games. Leaderboard participation may show your Play Games profile and score to other players under Google's settings. The game does not send your Play Games identity to our advertising integration.

Google Play processes payments for permanent unlocks, consumable Sigils and the optional Ranker's Pass subscription. The game receives product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state and subscription ownership information to verify, grant, acknowledge, consume and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card number or bank-account credentials. Pseudonymous account and saved-adventure references are used to associate eligible purchases with the correct player and adventure.

When advertisements are available and permitted by the consent system, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process network information such as IP address, approximate location inferred from it, ad/app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers. These support delivery, measurement, reliability and fraud prevention. The game requests non-personalized ads and excludes the Android advertising-ID permission from its current Android manifest. Other SDK identifiers and network data may still be processed. Non-personalized does not mean no data is collected.

When collection is permitted in an Analytics-enabled release, Google Analytics for Firebase reports app activity such as starts and sessions, an app-instance identifier, approximate location inferred from network information, and supported purchase or subscription events that can include the product and price. We use these reports to understand app use, measure advertising and improve the game. The game does not send Analytics player names, Play Games player IDs, saves or a developer-assigned user ID. We do not add custom gameplay events or custom copies of ad-impression events. Advertising-ID collection and ad personalization are disabled for this integration; other identifiers and network information can still be processed.

Advertisements and choices

Advertisements may appear after the player continues from an eligible expedition result. Optional rewarded advertisements display their reward before the player chooses to watch. Rewards are granted only after the provider reports that they were earned. Patron's Crest ownership and an active Ranker's Pass remove automatic ads and provide eligible reward claims without an ad, under the same reward limits.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request applicable consent and privacy choices before requesting ads. Where required, a Privacy choices control is available in Account & Privacy. Declining an available consent option does not prevent connected gameplay or remove base expedition rewards. Premium offline access is independent of consent choices. Ad availability depends on your region, choices, connectivity and the provider.

During first setup, you choose Under 16, 16–17, or 18 and older. This age-group choice is required to finish setup, including when your group receives no ads. Under-16 players receive no ads. Players aged 16–17 receive Google's teen ad treatment. You can change your age-group choice in Account & Privacy; it remains separate from your cloud save. Non-personalized requests apply to all age groups for which advertising is available.

New installs of Analytics-enabled releases start with Analytics collection disabled. The game enables collection only for an age group of 16 or older after its privacy flow reports that all required consent-mode purposes are granted or do not apply. Missing, declined, unknown or unconfigured purposes keep collection disabled. Valid permissions can persist across app launches until changed. Known age-group changes, account transitions, privacy changes and game resets disable collection while the game checks eligibility again. Where a Privacy choices control is available, use it to review your choices. Declining an available Analytics consent choice does not prevent connected gameplay.

Cloud saves and purchases

Cloud saves contain your selected game profile, including progress, inventory, settings and purchase/reward records needed for recovery. When connected, the game checks for cloud progress and backs up saved changes automatically at suitable breaks. An untouched installation or damaged local save can recover a valid cloud adventure automatically. If the device and cloud contain independent progress, the game asks which adventure to keep; balances are not merged. Replacing the device profile first creates a local recovery copy. Cloud backups can be older than your current device progress, so review the saves when a choice is offered.

Permanent purchase ownership can be restored through the same Google Play billing account. Consumable Sigils that have already been consumed by Play require a current game save to preserve their balance on another device. A Google Play Games account and a Google Play billing account can differ. Canceling a subscription, deleting game data or uninstalling the game are separate actions; uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Purchase verification and refunds

Dungeon Ranker uses a Shadedkin Studios service hosted on Google Cloud to verify eligible Google Play Sigil purchases and reconcile Sigil balances after verified Google Play refunds. Account-bound checks can run automatically after sign-in and when the game reconnects. This service keeps limited account and transaction records; it does not keep a second full adventure save. Purchases are optional.

The service uses a one-time Google Play Games authorization code to verify the player. It processes coded references to the player and saved adventure, Google Play purchase tokens and order identifiers, products, quantities, purchase and refund status, timestamps, and records of delivery and settlement. These records let us recover interrupted deliveries, apply confirmed refunds and prevent duplicate grants. A coded reference can still identify records belonging to the same account.

If Google Play refunds Sigils you have spent, your available balance can fall below zero. We record the amount deducted, any outstanding amount and repayments for that order. When a one-time gameplay reward repays a refund, we can retain up to eight hashed reward references and their repayment amounts per order. This lets you earn Sigils back through play and prevents an older save from clearing the amount owed or counting the same repayment twice.

Internal test versions can also submit a promotional test purchase for verification. For these claims the service can process the purchase token, product and available order information, the linked player and adventure, and records of validation, delivery and acknowledgement. We use these records to match a claim to its recipient and prevent repeated awards. Account deletion covers these test records, including claims from earlier versions.

Google authorization access and refresh tokens used to confirm the player are not stored by this service. The app uses a short-lived commerce session tied to the signed-in player; the server stores a hashed session reference and its expiry. Sessions currently expire after ten minutes. Signing out or expiry does not by itself erase records already stored by the service. Purchase verification and fulfillment are separate from advertising and Analytics consent. These commerce records are not used by this service for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Sharing and security

Data needed for these features is transmitted to Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, UMP and, when enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase. Google's services process information under their applicable terms, privacy policies and user controls. We do not sell your personal information directly or integrate a separate advertising SDK in this release. Google and its selected advertising partners may process advertising data under their own disclosures; available sale or sharing opt-out choices are presented through UMP. Google SDK service traffic uses encrypted network transport. No client-side storage or network service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google Cloud hosts and stores the limited commerce records on behalf of Shadedkin Studios. The Studio commerce database is hosted in Iowa, United States. The service exchanges the information necessary for verification with Google Play Games and Google Play's purchase services. Commerce requests use HTTPS, and server records are restricted to authorized service access. The application does not deliberately put purchase tokens, authorization codes or session credentials in its application logs. Hosting providers may process connection and request metadata, such as IP addresses and timestamps, to operate and secure their services. This does not mean that Shadedkin Studios receives a full copy of your Google account or payment details.

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, notification or shared-file access for these features. Network-derived approximate location does not require a GPS permission.

Delete your Dungeon Ranker account or request data deletion

By email. Send your request to shadedkinstudios@gmail.com. Tell us whether you want to close your Dungeon Ranker account or ask about particular data. You can send this request even if you have uninstalled the game. Please keep passwords, verification codes and payment details out of your message.

In the game. Open Account > Privacy > Delete hosted account while connected to the Play Games profile you want to close. Review the warning and confirm. Closing the Studio-hosted account is permanent. In the same screen, Check deletion status reports whether cleanup is pending or complete.

We verify control of the linked Play Games profile before closing its Studio account. If you have lost access to the game or device, email us to start your request. We will explain the verification options and the status of your request. If you have also lost access to the Google account, you can try Google account recovery. We can receive your request without a reinstall, but may be unable to complete closure if ownership cannot be verified. A display name, email address or Google Play order number alone cannot establish ownership.

Confirmation immediately blocks further Studio-hosted purchases from being verified for that profile. The service then deletes the account’s raw purchase tokens, order identifiers, saved-adventure links, sessions, historical gift claims and detailed refund records. Cleanup can continue after you leave the screen. When your adventure reconnects, the game removes unused purchased and gift Sigils that it tracks separately. You keep earned Sigils, progress and older balances that the save cannot distinguish.

To honor account closure and prevent duplicate credits, the service retains hashed account identifiers, hashed purchase references and any cumulative count of refunded units. A random deletion reference, request status and timestamps let the verified player check the result. Unlinked notification receipt hashes can also remain for security. These records have no scheduled expiry. Google manages its own account and payment records. You can manage your subscription in Google Play.

Other data and retention

Local progress, recovery copies and device records remain in the app's private storage. In-game reset starts a new local adventure after confirmation; it is not a promise to erase every retained recovery copy. If local saves are damaged or conflict, starting a new adventure through the recovery screen first preserves the old save directory on the device. Cloud restoration can also preserve a local copy of the previous adventure. These copies support recovery and are not sent to Shadedkin Studios.

Android's clear-app-data operation removes this app's local progress and recovery material. Applicable uninstall behavior can also remove app data. Back up progress you want to keep first. Resetting, clearing data or uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions or erase Google's purchase records.

Cloud data is controlled separately from local data. In Account & Privacy, Start over offers an optional Also delete my Google cloud adventure choice when Google Play Games is connected. Cloud deletion needs an internet connection and confirmation from Google before the local reset proceeds. Leaving this choice off keeps the cloud backup. Another device can upload its saved adventure again later.

You can also use Google's controls to delete this game's Play Games data. Instructions are at Google Play Games data deletion. Deleting cloud data does not clear local saves on your devices; remove those separately if desired. Google controls retention of payment records, advertising data, service diagnostics and other Google account information under its policies.

A local game reset or uninstall does not by itself delete Analytics information already sent to Google. Uploaded Analytics information is subject to Google Analytics retention and deletion controls. Contact us for help with an Analytics privacy request; local game saves, Google Play Games cloud data and Google purchase records remain separate.

Shadedkin Studios retains purchase, historical reward and refund records while they are needed to support eligible transactions, process later refunds, prevent repeated claims and meet applicable legal obligations. A new adventure, local reset, cloud-save deletion, clearing app data, uninstalling, signing out or session expiry does not by itself erase these records.

For other privacy requests or help identifying the appropriate control, contact shadedkinstudios@gmail.com. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include; do not send passwords, full payment details or government identity documents.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask for access to your personal data, a copy in a portable format, correction or deletion. You may also ask us to restrict processing, object to processing or withdraw consent you gave. Email shadedkinstudios@gmail.com to exercise a right. Where applicable, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For players in the European Economic Area, we process account and purchase information as needed to provide the game and transactions you request. We have a legitimate interest in protecting accounts, detecting duplicate or fraudulent claims, and securing the service. We honor legal obligations to retain records where they apply. Where consent is required for advertising or Analytics, you can change your choice in the game's privacy controls. Withdrawing consent applies to future processing.

Your information may be processed outside the country where you live. The database for our commerce service is in Iowa in the United States. Google describes the transfer mechanisms it offers for Google Cloud, including standard contractual clauses where applicable, in its Cloud Data Processing Addendum. You can email us for information about safeguards that apply to your data or to request a copy.

Audience and changes

Dungeon Ranker is a dark-fantasy game intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Advertising is configured for a teen/adult audience.

We may update this policy when features, providers or data practices change. Questions can be sent to Shadedkin Studios at shadedkinstudios@gmail.com.
