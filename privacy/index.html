Dungeon Ranker

Privacy Policy

Developer: Shadedkin Studios
Effective date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dungeon Ranker handles information when you use the Android game and its optional Google Play services, purchases, and rewarded advertising.

Dungeon Ranker is designed to remain fully playable without purchasing anything or watching rewarded advertisements. The game does not use a custom account server, custom analytics backend, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics at launch.

1. Information we handle

Depending on which optional features you use, Dungeon Ranker and the Google services integrated into the game may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Approximate location: Google Mobile Ads may use an IP address to estimate general location.
  • Name and user IDs: if you use Google Play Games, the game may receive your Play Games display name/gamertag and a Google Play Games player identifier.
  • Purchase history: Google Play Billing provides information needed to confirm, grant, and restore permanent purchases. Dungeon Ranker does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.
  • App interactions: when optional rewarded ads are used, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Other gameplay actions: if you use Google Play Games features, gameplay-service data such as leaderboard scores, achievement progress, and Saved Games progress may be sent to Google Play Games.
  • Diagnostics: Google services may process diagnostic information used to maintain service reliability and performance.
  • Device or other identifiers: Google Mobile Ads may process identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and related device or account identifiers.

2. How information is used

Information is used only for the purposes associated with the feature or service involved, including:

  • providing optional Google Play Games functionality such as leaderboards, achievements, and Saved Games;
  • granting and restoring permanent in-app purchases;
  • providing optional rewarded video advertising;
  • measuring advertising and service performance;
  • fraud prevention, security, abuse prevention, and compliance;
  • service diagnostics and reliability.

3. Advertising and consent

Dungeon Ranker uses optional rewarded video advertising through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Rewarded advertisements are not required for normal gameplay, progression, characters, leaderboards, or completion of the game.

The game uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage required privacy and consent choices before Mobile Ads is allowed to request an advertisement. If consent or privacy requirements prevent advertising, the game remains playable and base rewards remain available.

Because Dungeon Ranker targets players aged 13 and older and does not collect or verify date of birth at launch, advertising is configured conservatively for a teen/adult audience rather than a child-directed audience.

4. Google Play Games

Google Play Games features are optional. If you use them, Google may process your Play Games identity and game-service activity for features such as leaderboards, achievements, and Saved Games. Dungeon Ranker does not create or operate a separate Shadedkin Studios account system.

5. In-app purchases

Dungeon Ranker uses Google Play Billing for permanent, non-consumable digital products. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Dungeon Ranker may receive purchase and entitlement information needed to grant, acknowledge, restore, or prevent duplicate granting of purchased content. The game does not receive your full credit-card number or bank-account credentials.

6. Data sharing and service providers

Dungeon Ranker does not sell personal information. Data may be transmitted to Google services that provide Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, UMP consent, and related platform functionality. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share certain data, including approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers, for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Shadedkin Studios does not use a custom advertising network, third-party analytics service, social network SDK, custom account backend, or custom hosted player database at launch.

7. Local data and Saved Games

Most Dungeon Ranker progression is stored locally in the app's private storage. If you choose to use Google Play Games Saved Games, an optional game-progress snapshot may also be stored through Google Play Games for restoration and conflict-resolution purposes.

8. Permissions

Dungeon Ranker is not designed to request microphone, camera, contacts, precise location, notification, or user-file/storage permissions for its launch functionality. Approximate location described above may be inferred by Google Mobile Ads from network information such as IP address and does not require Dungeon Ranker to request GPS permission.

9. Security

Network communications used by the integrated Google services are expected to use encryption in transit. Shadedkin Studios limits the game's launch data flows to the platform services needed for the features described in this policy.

10. Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on the device until it is removed through app data clearing, uninstall/reinstall behavior, or an in-game deletion/reset feature when available. Google-managed information, such as Play Games data, purchase records, advertising data, and service diagnostics, is retained and handled according to the applicable Google service policies and controls.

Dungeon Ranker does not maintain a custom account database that can be independently deleted by Shadedkin Studios. Where Google provides controls for Play Games or advertising data, those controls are managed through the relevant Google account or privacy settings.

11. Children and target audience

Dungeon Ranker is not directed to children under 13. The planned Google Play target age groups are 13–15, 16–17, and 18+. The game is designed and presented as a teen/adult dark-fantasy product.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Dungeon Ranker adds, removes, or changes SDKs, platform services, permissions, data practices, or legal requirements. The effective date above will be updated when a material revision is published.
