Dungeon Ranker Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026.

Developer: Shadedkin Studios. Contact: shadedkinstudios@gmail.com.

Dungeon Ranker stores your adventure on your device and connects to Google Play Games for cloud saves, rankings and achievements. The Android version requires an internet connection and Google Play Games sign-in during first setup. Ordinary gameplay also requires internet after setup. Permanent Patron's Crest owners can play offline. An active Ranker's Pass includes offline play for up to 72 hours after Google Play verification. Internet is needed to purchase or restore these benefits on a device. The game reads Android's connection status to apply this rule; it does not send that status to a Shadedkin Studios server. Google Play purchases are optional, and advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads when available. Internal test versions use test advertisements. We do not operate a separate Shadedkin Studios login service, hosted player database, custom analytics backend, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in this release.

Information and purposes

Your local save includes heroes, equipment, currencies, gameplay progress, settings and records needed to avoid granting a purchase or reward twice. Device-local records also limit advertisement frequency and record pending ad rewards. These records are used to operate the game and recover interrupted actions. An advertising age group is stored only on this device; we do not ask for your birthday. The advertising SDK receives the applicable teen/adult treatment when ads are permitted, rather than an exact age or birth date.

When Google Play Games is used, Google may provide a player identifier and display name. Cloud backup sends a game-progress snapshot to Google Play Games. The game uses this identity to keep cloud choices associated with the correct player. When connected, earned leaderboard scores and achievement progress may also be sent to Google Play Games. Leaderboard participation may show your Play Games profile and score to other players under Google's settings. The game does not send your Play Games identity to our advertising integration.

Google Play processes payments for permanent unlocks, consumable Sigils and the optional Ranker's Pass subscription. The game receives product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state and subscription ownership information to verify, grant, acknowledge, consume and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card number or bank-account credentials. A local profile identifier is hashed before it is supplied to Play Billing for purchase association.

When advertisements are available and permitted by the consent system, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process network information such as IP address, approximate location inferred from it, ad/app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers. These support delivery, measurement, reliability and fraud prevention. The game requests non-personalized ads and excludes the Android advertising-ID permission from its current Android manifest. Other SDK identifiers and network data may still be processed. Non-personalized does not mean no data is collected.

Advertisements and choices

Advertisements may appear after the player continues from an eligible expedition result. Optional rewarded advertisements display their reward before the player chooses to watch. Rewards are granted only after the provider reports that they were earned. Patron's Crest ownership and an active Ranker's Pass remove automatic ads and provide eligible reward claims without an ad, under the same reward limits.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request applicable consent and privacy choices before requesting ads. Where required, a Privacy choices control is available in Account & Privacy. Declining an available consent option does not prevent connected gameplay or remove base expedition rewards. Premium offline access is independent of consent choices. Ad availability depends on your region, choices, connectivity and the provider.

During first setup, you choose Under 16, 16–17, or 18 and older. This age-group choice is required to finish setup, including when your group receives no ads. Under-16 players receive no ads. Players aged 16–17 receive Google's teen ad treatment. You can change your age-group choice in Account & Privacy; it remains separate from your cloud save. Non-personalized requests apply to all age groups for which advertising is available.

Cloud saves and purchases

Cloud saves contain your selected game profile, including progress, inventory, settings and purchase/reward records needed for recovery. When connected, the game checks for cloud progress and backs up saved changes automatically at suitable breaks. An untouched installation or damaged local save can recover a valid cloud adventure automatically. If the device and cloud contain independent progress, the game asks which adventure to keep; balances are not merged. Replacing the device profile first creates a local recovery copy. Cloud backups can be older than your current device progress, so review the saves when a choice is offered.

Permanent purchase ownership can be restored through the same Google Play billing account. Consumable Sigils that have already been consumed by Play require a current game save to preserve their balance on another device. A Google Play Games account and a Google Play billing account can differ. Canceling a subscription, deleting game data or uninstalling the game are separate actions; uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Sharing and security

Data needed for these features is transmitted to Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Google's services process information under their applicable terms, privacy policies and user controls. We do not sell your personal information directly or integrate a separate advertising SDK in this release. Google and its selected advertising partners may process advertising data under their own disclosures; available sale or sharing opt-out choices are presented through UMP. Google SDK service traffic uses encrypted network transport. No client-side storage or network service can be guaranteed completely secure.

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, notification or shared-file access for these features. Network-derived approximate location does not require a GPS permission.

Retention and deletion

Local progress, recovery copies and device records remain in the app's private storage. In-game reset starts a new local adventure after confirmation; it is not a promise to erase every retained recovery copy. If local saves are damaged or conflict, starting a new adventure through the recovery screen first preserves the old save directory on the device. Cloud restoration can also preserve a local copy of the previous adventure. These copies support recovery and are not sent to Shadedkin Studios.

Android's clear-app-data operation removes this app's local progress and recovery material. Applicable uninstall behavior can also remove app data. Back up progress you want to keep first. Resetting, clearing data or uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions or erase Google's purchase records.

Cloud data is controlled separately from local data. In Account & Privacy, Start over offers an optional Also delete my Google cloud adventure choice when Google Play Games is connected. Cloud deletion needs an internet connection and confirmation from Google before the local reset proceeds. Leaving this choice off keeps the cloud backup. Another device can upload its saved adventure again later.

You can also use Google's controls to delete this game's Play Games data. Instructions are at Google Play Games data deletion. Deleting cloud data does not clear local saves on your devices; remove those separately if desired. Google controls retention of payment records, advertising data, service diagnostics and other Google account information under its policies.

For privacy requests or help identifying the appropriate control, contact shadedkinstudios@gmail.com. We do not maintain a separate hosted player account database. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include; do not send passwords, full payment details or government identity documents.

Audience and changes

Dungeon Ranker is a dark-fantasy game intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Advertising is configured for a teen/adult audience.

We may update this policy when features, providers or data practices change. A published revision will show its effective date. Questions can be sent to Shadedkin Studios at shadedkinstudios@gmail.com.
