Squire: Behind the Banner Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.
Developer: Shadedkin Studios
Contact: shadedkinstudios@gmail.com

Squire: Behind the Banner stores your company, journey and tales on your device. Its optional Android advertising and measurement features use Google Mobile Ads, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Analytics for Firebase. These services can process technical information and identifiers when used, even when ads are non-personalized.

This policy covers the current internal test and its Android integration. Test advertising is configured; live rewarded advertisements and survival-aid rewards are disabled. Some test builds may not include working Android services. The game does not currently provide player accounts, cloud saves, chat, public player posts, real-money purchases or subscriptions.

Information kept on your device

The game stores the information needed to play, save a result once and recover interrupted actions. This includes your chosen character names, party choices and appearance, equipment, supplies, progress, Renown, earned cosmetic ownership, relationships, story events and tales. It also keeps accepted-action history, checkpoints and archived expedition records. Starting another tale preserves the previous expedition rather than deleting every record.

Game settings and privacy preferences are stored locally. For advertising eligibility, you can choose Under 16, 16–17, or 18 or older; an unset choice remains unknown. The game does not ask for your birthday. The age band is a device preference, and eligible ad requests apply the relevant teen/adult treatment rather than sending an exact birth date.

Shadedkin Studios does not receive your local saves or character names through this integration. The game has no separate Shadedkin Studios sign-in, hosted player database, Firebase Authentication or Firebase Crashlytics integration. It does not provide its own cross-device backup or restore service.

Google privacy, advertising and measurement services

Privacy choices

For eligible players who choose to review consent, the app contacts Google's UMP service to check which privacy message applies and to handle available choices. This involves a network request and technical information needed to deliver the form, along with consent and preference records. A consent screen is not a promise that no information is processed before you make a choice: the privacy service itself must communicate with Google.

Advertising

When the age and privacy checks permit an ad request, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process the device's IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app/ad interactions, performance diagnostics, app set identifiers and other device or account identifiers supported by the SDK. Google uses such information for ad delivery, measurement, reliability and fraud prevention. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

The integration requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher ad personalization, applies teen treatment for the 16–17 band, and sets a maximum ad content rating of T. It is configured to remove the Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions. Removing advertising-ID access does not remove other SDK identifiers or network information. Non-personalized does not mean no data is collected.

The current internal test uses test ad requests. Optional rewarded videos and rewarded survival aid are not active. Ad availability depends on the build, your choices, network access and Google's response; a skipped or unavailable ad is not queued as a later gameplay popup.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Analytics measurement starts disabled. It may be enabled for players aged 16 or older only after the current privacy flow reports all purposes required by this test's consent gate as granted or not applicable. Missing, declined, unknown or unconfigured purposes keep measurement disabled. Advertising personalization and Analytics advertising-ID collection remain disabled.

When enabled, automatic Analytics reports can include app opens, sessions and duration, device model and operating system, language, approximate geography derived from network information, and a generated app-instance identifier. We use these reports to understand app use and improve the game. We do not send character names, tale text, game saves, a developer-assigned user ID or custom player-text events to Analytics. See Google's Analytics data collection information and collection controls.

Your choices

Open Privacy & ads from the game's title menu to choose an age band or use Review consent choices. Where Google requires an additional privacy form, Privacy options opens it. Available options vary by region and Google's message configuration. Ad permission and Analytics permission are checked separately; being able to request an ad does not by itself enable Analytics.

The integration is configured not to start new ad or consent requests, or enable Analytics measurement, while the age choice is unknown or under 16. Eligible players explicitly start the consent review. Choosing not to start these optional services does not remove local progress or base expedition rewards.

Changing the age band or privacy choices clears prepared ads and disables measurement while eligibility is checked again. Previously initialized third-party components may remain loaded, and previously transmitted information is not erased by that change. A declined option does not imply that every Google service stops processing all technical information; the app requests ads only when the current privacy result permits it.

Recipients, security and permissions

The optional services communicate with Google and, where applicable, its service providers or advertising partners under their own terms, settings and disclosures. We do not upload or sell your local saves or character names through these features. Google's advertising data practices and available opt-out choices are separate from your device's game records. More information is available in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Google documents encrypted transport for its SDK service traffic. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. The game features described here do not require your camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS location. Approximate location derived from a network request can be processed without a GPS permission.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any material you choose to attach so we can respond. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or government identity documents.

Retention, deletion and requests

Local progress, archived tales, accepted-action history and recovery copies remain in the app's device storage until that data is removed. Starting a new tale does not erase the archive. Android's clear-app-storage controls remove the app's local data on that installation, including preferences and recovery material. Uninstall and operating-system backup/restore behavior may affect what remains or returns; the game itself does not promise reinstall or cross-device recovery.

Changing a preference, clearing local data or uninstalling does not by itself delete information already sent to Google. Google service records are subject to the relevant service's retention and deletion controls; Analytics information is also subject to the project's configured Analytics retention settings. There is no Shadedkin Studios player account to delete in this test.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information. For a privacy request, contact shadedkinstudios@gmail.com. Tell us which app and service your request concerns. We will explain what we can identify or act on and help you find Google's applicable controls when needed. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant request; do not include sensitive identity documents in an initial email.

Audience

Squire: Behind the Banner is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The age-band choice is not identity verification. The internal integration's under-16 setting disables ad requests and Analytics measurement as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.
